Verify your download

We publish a SHA-256 checksum so you can confirm the installer hasn’t been tampered with.

PowerShell

Get-FileHash .\SigStreamAgent-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256

Compare the result to the SHA-256 shown above. They must match exactly.

Command Prompt

certutil -hashfile SigStreamAgent-Setup.exe SHA256
