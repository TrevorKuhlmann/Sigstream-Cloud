FAQ

Does the agent handle flaky networks?

Yes—telemetry queues locally and flushes on reconnect with backoff and retries.

Is it hobbyist‑friendly?

Absolutely. Low monthly cost and a generous free tier to tinker.

How do I get live data?

Use GET /devapi/stream?api_key=... and listen with EventSource.

Is it tenant‑aware?

Yes—per‑device keys map to owners. REST & SSE queries are scoped accordingly.
